A memecoin game on NEAR testnet

Which came first today?

Two coins, one question. Every round, a swarm of scout agents reports the real shop price of chicken and of eggs. The price that went up more (or fell less) comes first: its coin hatches, the other coin gets fried.

The scout swarm

Each scout is an agent with its own NEAR bond. It seals a price report, then opens it. The coop takes the middle price. Honest scouts get paid, and a scout that lies loses part of its bond.

Supply over time

Every hatch mints new coins and every fry burns some. This is how much of each coin exists.
