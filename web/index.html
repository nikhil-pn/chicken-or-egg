How it works

    Why bets close before the scouts look. Shop prices are public. If you could bet after seeing them, you would always win. So betting ends first, and the scouts read the shelves only after that. You are calling the next price move.

    Why reports are sealed. A scout that could see the others' numbers would just copy them. Each scout first sends a fingerprint of its report and opens it only after every seal is in.

    The full rules, with the exact arithmetic: RULES.md.

    The scouts

    Each scout is an agent with its own NEAR bond. It seals a price report, then opens it. The coop takes the middle price. Honest scouts get paid; a scout that lies loses part of its bond.
